Odoo.

Web Designer (APAC) · Odoo HK Ltd.February 2023–present

At Odoo, I’ve led the website work on approximately 55 ERP projects. Clients have been primarily based in Australia, with others in New Zealand, Hong Kong and Southeast Asia.

For design projects, I shape website and eCommerce interfaces around each client’s industry, business model and daily operations. I present designs and implement changes based on client feedback.

I explain platform trade-offs and new capabilities, and train clients to maintain their websites using the Odoo website builder.

Solution Forest.

Web Programmer · Solution Forest Ltd.August 2019–March 2020

At Solution Forest, I developed and maintained client websites and content management systems using HTML, CSS/SCSS, Bootstrap, WordPress and Laravel.

I built responsive layouts across browsers and devices, and worked with teammates to clarify client requirements and resolve technical issues.

Timeline, budget
and quality.

I help clients weigh their ideas against the time and budget available, and the quality they expect.

What a request involves

I look at a request as both a designer and a developer. That helps me explain the visual and technical work involved.

What fits the scope

I discuss how a change affects the time and budget needed to achieve the quality we’re aiming for.

What we agree to deliver

We agree on a workable scope and clear expectations for delivery.
